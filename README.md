# SOHAN City Partner & Regional Micro-Hub Landing Page

A high-converting, multi-section recruitment landing page built with **React, Vite, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS** for the **SOHAN City Partner Program**.

---

## Architecture & Sections
1. **Header:** Sticky navigation with SOHAN broken-disc mark, cohort status badge, and smooth-scroll CTAs.
2. **Hero:** High-impact value proposition (*"Own the SOHAN Fragrance Network in Your City"*), dual-engine hooks, and trust anchors.
3. **Olfactory Universe (Products):** Visual breakdown of № 01 Saffron Pistachio, № 02 Burnt Sugar, № 03 Sesame Skin, and the Discovery Set tripwire, highlighting retail MSRP vs. wholesale margins.
4. **The Dual-Engine Model:** Side-by-side breakdown of Active Direct Retail (45% margin) and Passive Ad Micro-Fulfillment ($5.00/order bounty).
5. **The 60-Second "Pre-Kitted" Workflow:** 5-step operational timeline demonstrating how pre-assembled luxury boxes eliminate packaging friction.
6. **Interactive Earnings Calculator:** Live sliders allowing prospects to simulate monthly direct sales and ad routing orders.
7. **Partner Tiers & Starter Packs:** Three investment tiers ($1,200, $3,500, $8,000) with detailed inclusions and territory scopes.
8. **Trust & FTC Compliance:** 90% Buy-Back Guarantee, zero MLM downlines, and territory protection terms.
9. **Frequently Asked Questions:** Interactive accordion resolving shipping postage, ad underperformance, and storage requirements.
10. **Territory Application Form:** Multi-field qualification form with state validation, tier selection, and confirmation state.
11. **Footer:** European brand heritage (Poznań, Poland), legal governance, and contact channels.
